# Retail-Sales-Analytics-Power-BI
Interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze retail sales, profit performance, order trends, and regional insights using DAX, Power Query, and data visualization.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. KPI Overview
Provides a high-level overview of key business performance metrics, including:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Average Order Value

### 2. Sales Performance
Analyzes sales trends and overall revenue performance across different business dimensions.

### 3. Product & Profitability Analysis
Evaluates product performance, profitability, category contribution, and sales-versus-profit relationships.

### 4. Customer & Order Analysis
Analyzes customer segments, order patterns, shipping methods, and category-level performance.

**Visualizations included:**
- Orders by Customer Segment
- Sales Distribution by Category
- Customer Segment by Region
- Monthly Order Trend
- Orders by Shipping Mode
- Sales Performance by Segment Over Time
- Profit by Category

### 5. Executive Summary & Business Insights
Provides an interactive overview of overall business performance.

**Key components:**
- Five KPI Cards
- Sales Drivers Analysis using Decomposition Tree
- Regional Sales & Profit Analysis using Matrix
- Key Business Insights
- Interactive slicers for Order Date, Region, and Product Category

**Project Objectives**
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs) and order trends.
- Explore regional and product-level sales performance.
- Identify patterns and relationships through interactive visualizations.

**Tools & Technologies**
- Power BI – Dashboard development and reporting
- Power Query – Data preparation and transformation
- DAX – Measures and KPI calculations
- Data Visualization – Interactive charts, cards, and filters

**Key Features**
- KPI cards for sales, profit, orders, and other business metrics.
- Sales trend and regional performance analysis.
- Product-level and profitability insights.
- Interactive visuals for exploring the data.

**Project File**

The .pbix file is available in this repository. Download it and open it using Power BI Desktop to explore the report.

Data Analysis | Data Cleaning | Power Query | DAX | KPI Reporting | Dashboard Design | Data Visualization
